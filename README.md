# AERIUM 1.1.1 ( BETA )
### Give Your Acoustic Guitar a More Natural Sound

**A completely free acoustic guitar plugin.**

Ever recorded your acoustic guitar directly into your computer and felt that it sounded a little too much like a pickup?

That's exactly why I started working on **Aerium**.

Aerium is an acoustic guitar plugin designed to help bring back some of the warmth, depth and natural character of a guitar recorded with microphones.

Whether you're recording covers, writing your own songs or simply playing around with different sounds, Aerium gives you more control over your acoustic guitar tone without needing an expensive recording setup.

## What Can Aerium Do?

** More Natural Acoustic Guitar Sounds**

Aerium uses microphone impulse responses (IRs) to help your guitar's pickup sound closer to a mic'ed acoustic guitar.

**🎙️ Experiment With Different Mic Sounds**

Choose from a collection of microphone sounds, adjust their placement and blend them with your guitar's original pickup signal.

**🎛️ Shape Your Tone**

Add warmth, adjust your EQ, control dynamics and experiment with effects such as chorus, shimmer and reverb.

**🎵 Made for Recording and Creating**

Whether you're a fingerstyle guitarist, singer-songwriter or someone who enjoys recording at home, Aerium is designed to be easy to use and fun to experiment with.

## Completely Free for Everyone!

Yes, Aerium is **100% free to use**.

This is a personal project I've been working on, and I wanted to create something useful that I could share with fellow guitarists and the music community.

## Requirements and installation

Aerium currently supports Mac only. Sorry to my Windows friends! A Windows version is something I plan to work on in the future.

Aerium is currently in beta testing, with the final release planned before Christmas 2026.

The plugin is already functional, but as this is still a beta, you may encounter occasional bugs or features that don't behave as expected.

**What you'll need:**

- A Mac running macOS 12 or later
- Recording software such as Logic Pro, GarageBand or Ableton Live
- An audio interface
- An acoustic guitar with a pickup system

1. Read the illustrated guide, save your work and close your audio hosts.
2. Download `Aerium-1.1.1-macOS.pkg` and compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. Open the installer and choose VST3, Audio Unit, or both under Customise. Complete the normal administrator authentication.
4. Reopen your DAW and load **Aerium**. Use VST3 in Ableton Live or AU in Logic Pro/GarageBand. Rescan plugins if necessary.

The installer is **unsigned and unnotarised**. Its plugins are ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed. If macOS blocks this installer, follow the screenshots in the guide and Apple's [per-installer opening instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) only if you trust the source and have verified the download. Do not disable Gatekeeper or override an explicit malware detection.
